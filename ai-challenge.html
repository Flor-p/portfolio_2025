My workflow

Three layers built before the event, deployed live under pressure.

01 — Engine prep

I tuned JSON Prompt Engine for the challenge format: roulette brief, 10-minute clock, video with audio. Standard A = Seedance 2.0 Fast (15 s, 3 shots, 720p, audio on); Standard B = Veo 3.1 Fast (8 s). The AI assistant was updated with multi-shot timing, creative IDEA step, Spanish dialogue, physically described Argentine objects (mate con bombilla), and correct animal anatomy.

02 — Error guards

Automated guards in the code: clean avoid lists, no real people or copyrighted characters (auto-replaced with original archetypes), no empty strings on screen, wrong-language dialogue auto-deleted. Timed rehearsals at 5:02 with the engine and 6:00 with the chat, plus credit control for Magnific and Anthropic.

03 — Plan B: the DUELO chat

A Claude session connected to Magnific via MCP, with a dedicated method doc: type DUELO: <brief> → get IDEA + Seedance-ready prompt, with shortcuts B, GUION, MÁS LOCO, MÁS EMOCIÓN, MÁS HUMOR. This is what ran on stage.
